# Original 5500 1.2.8.24: full control restored, 29 October 2026

> **Evidence status:** static (the 5500 firmware 1.2.8.24 and `5500PC.exe` 1.2.8.24, see [M3](2026-10-28-m3-5500.md) and [protocol notes](../protocol/5500.md)), exercised end to end against the scripted 5500 unit in `crates/orban-web` (its own opcodes, `5500.50` documents, login without a session line). No 5500 firmware or hardware was available or touched. Firmware/hardware acceptance is deferred to the final stage of Phase 10.

## Change

`605684c` made the 5500 adapter `READ_ONLY`. The owner reversed that: the 5500 adapter is `STATIC_FULL_WITH_PRESET_FILES` again, with `WIRE_5500` (the 5500 firmware's own switch: EEh/EFh/F1h/F2h/F3h, ECh delete, F9h→FAh). Nothing of the 5500i's wire or profile is used for it. The `require_writes` guard from `605684c` stays: it now protects every change path for any adapter that cannot write.
